OBLIQUE HOUSE / ALEX LAURENT - DESIGN SYSTEM
===========================================
Desktop reference: artboard 1440 px wide. All values in CSS px.
Measured from the layout PDF. CSS variables: design-tokens.css (same values).


1. PRODUCT STRUCTURE
--------------------
"My Space" is a private member portal. The 7 layouts are one user journey,
shown in this order (the bottom progress bar has one segment per step):

  01  My Space ................ member profile, current State, mantra, birth date / zodiac / city
  02  Your Chosen World ....... recommended hotel (Oblique House), experiences, scent, Book your stay
  03  Scent Invitation ........ the scented card, Order my card, card delivery status
                                 (Preparing / Sent / Delivered)
  04  My Intention ............ two editable fields: "I am becoming" + intention text
  05  My Ritual ............... perfume oil ritual, Read the steps / Open my perfume oil ritual
  06  My Community - locked ... community text, Plan your stay, access-code entry
  07  My Community - unlocked . same screen after a valid access code: property circle
                                 (Oblique House), Event / Guest invitation / News cards

Screens 06 and 07 are two STATES of the same page, not two pages.

4.13 Card delivery status (screen 03)
